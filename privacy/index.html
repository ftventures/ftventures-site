Privacy notice

Last updated 13 November 2026

This notice explains how ftventures.io handles personal data. In short: the site collects nothing about you, and the only personal data I hold is what you choose to send me.

Who is responsible

This website is operated by Oasis Ventures FZE, OneUAQ, Umm Al Quwain Free Trade Zone, UAE, which is responsible for any personal data described here. FT Ventures is not yet a separate legal entity. Contact: felipe@ftventures.io

What this site does not do

  • No cookies, analytics, tracking pixels or advertising.
  • No forms. The site does not ask you for any information.
  • Fonts are hosted on this site, so your browser does not contact Google or other font providers.

What is collected, and why

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address to deliver and secure the site. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Email. If you email me, I use your name, address and message only to reply and, where relevant, to work with you.
  • Booking a call. The "book a call" link opens Google Calendar's booking page. Google processes the details you enter there, and I receive your name, email and any notes so I can hold the meeting.

I rely on legitimate interest (replying to you and arranging meetings) or on steps you ask me to take before entering into an agreement. Nothing on this site is an offer of securities, and contacting me does not sign you up to anything.

How long it is kept

Emails and meeting details are kept for as long as needed for the conversation or working relationship, and deleted when no longer needed, unless the law requires otherwise.

Sharing

Your data is not sold or shared for marketing. It is only processed by the service providers above (GitHub, Google), which may store data outside your country under their own safeguards.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU or UK GDPR or the UAE data protection law), you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to how it is used. Email felipe@ftventures.io and I will respond within a month. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top will be updated.
