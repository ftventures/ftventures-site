Fractional CFO02

Burn to profitability.
Fundraise to exit.

Fractional CFO for high-growth, VC- and PE-backed companies, from B2B SaaS to consumer brands.

I build finance functions, sharpen commercial decision-making and help founders through fundraising, profitability and M&A. Two exits as an operator, and multiple finance functions built from the ground up.

Most growing companies hit the same finance ceiling

Before

  • Founder-led finance
  • Ad-hoc models
  • Reactive reporting
  • Decisions driven by intuition
  • Finance seen as “support”

As you scale

  • Board scrutiny increases
  • Investors want clarity
  • Capital allocation decisions matter
  • Weak finance becomes visible very fast

The problem

  • Numbers exist, but aren’t trusted
  • Board conversations become defensive
  • Metrics don’t reconcile across decks
  • Forecasts feel optimistic, not controlled
  • The CEO absorbs unnecessary finance load

The company has outgrown founder-led finance, but isn’t ready to hire a full-time CFO.

Where I'm useful

Growth has outpaced the finance function

Reporting, forecasting and commercial decision-making need to catch up. I set up budgeting, FP&A, treasury, tax, legal and systems, then hand over to a controller I help you hire.

Burn is too high

The board wants a credible path to break-even without sacrificing growth. Unit economics, cohorts, pricing and cost architecture.

A fundraise is 6–12 months away

The model, materials, data room and process need CFO ownership. $30M+ raised across Series A and B, plus bridge and debt financing.

An exit is on the horizon

You need someone who has run a sell-side process in-house: buyer engagement, diligence, negotiation and post-merger integration.

Board and investors need more

Board packs, KPI architecture, annual operating plans and long-range planning. Reporting into EU/US shareholders and PE-owned acquirers.

Senior CFO capability, not a full-time CFO

Typically post-Series A/B, 2–3 days a week is enough. Remote-first with on-site presence for board meetings, fundraising and critical periods.
